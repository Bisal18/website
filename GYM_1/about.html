Built On Obsession

NeonForge started in 2014 as a single studio in Jubilee Hills. Today it's Hyderabad's most advanced training facility.

Our Story

We were tired of gyms that felt like warehouses with treadmills. NeonForge was built to feel like the future — biometric feedback, immersive lighting, and equipment that actually pushes you further. What began as a 2,000 sq. ft. studio has grown into a 15,000 sq. ft. facility with three specialized training zones.

Every piece of equipment, every class format, and every trainer on our floor is chosen for one reason: results you can measure.
