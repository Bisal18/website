Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Reach out and we'll respond within a day.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No. All plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. Cancel or upgrade anytime from your account.

Is the first trial actually free?+

Yes — new members get 3 days of full floor and class access completely free. Just book it on the Contact page.

What are your operating hours?+

Monday–Saturday 5 AM to 10 PM, Sunday 7 AM to 1 PM (open floor access only, no scheduled classes).

Do you offer personal training?+

Yes. Elite members get 4 sessions/month included, and Basic/Pro members can add sessions à la carte — ask at the front desk.

Can I bring a guest?+

Elite members get 2 guest passes per month. Basic and Pro members can purchase a day pass for guests at the desk.

Is there parking available?+

Yes, free covered parking for all members directly beneath the Jubilee Hills facility.

How do I switch or upgrade my plan?+

Message us on the Contact page or speak to the front desk — upgrades take effect on your next billing cycle.
